Methodology, limitations & verification

1 · Geographic coverage

The live source is the Countries States Cities Database, release family current to July 29, 2026: 250 country/territory records, 5,308 first-order regions/municipalities, and 152,970 cities/towns/districts. “All cities” means all records in that maintained source; no global settlement database can guarantee every inhabited place on Earth.

2 · Urgency evidence

Country urgency uses INFORM Risk 2026, a structural humanitarian/disaster risk index combining hazards & exposure, vulnerability, and lack of coping capacity. The app fetches a CORS-enabled mirror of the EC/JRC figures and shows its release date. This is not a live emergency alert or travel advisory.

3 · Subnational integrity

States and cities inherit the national baseline unless a separate verified subnational layer is available. Their recommendations may change with scale, latitude, and live country indicators, but the app does not fabricate local risk scores.

4 · Live-context integrity

Provider adapters preserve source, fetch time, cache state, and geographic scope. Country-only datasets are explicitly labeled as national context when viewed beneath a state or city; point/radius datasets are labeled as vicinity data rather than jurisdiction-wide measurements.

5 · Project ranking

Each archetype has transparent weights for hazard, vulnerability, coping-capacity gaps, development indicators, and planning scale. Scores rank project exploration priority only. They are not cost-benefit estimates, engineering approvals, or political recommendations.

6 · Zero-harm / anti-inversion

Projects should increase local capability without coercion, displacement, hidden surveillance, or dependency. In conflict-sensitive settings, do not publish precise coordinates of vulnerable people or critical assets. Use local consent, safeguarding, accessibility, maintenance funding, and independent verification.

7 · Offline behavior

The HTML contains an offline ISO country/subdivision fallback. City records are loaded from the maintained browser package when online and stored in IndexedDB after access or explicit caching. A first-ever offline launch cannot retrieve cities that were never cached; the UI states that limitation instead of pretending otherwise.

Contextual synthesis methodology

Context before projects. The engine assembles a PlaceContext from loaded live sources, geographic scale, transparent heuristics, and optional local overrides. It then detects active environmental/human-system tags, filters incompatible archetypes, applies local adaptation notes, ranks candidates through the selected portfolio lens, and diversifies the displayed portfolio.

No hidden AI dependency. The standalone build implements the chain deterministically in JavaScript. A versioned meta-prompt registry can be copied for an external/local model, but no model is silently contacted. Missing data remains missing. Heuristic biome/coastal/settlement classifications are labeled and can be overridden by better local knowledge.

Rejection audit. Specialized archetypes may require conditions such as coastal exposure, aridity, high elevation, cold climate, flood risk, seismic activity, remoteness, or urban form. Candidates that fail a required condition remain inspectable in the rejection audit rather than being silently removed.
